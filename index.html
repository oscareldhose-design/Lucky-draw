    How it works

    1. 1Add people

      Type a name, phone number and email in Entries (all three are required), paste a list, or import a CSV. Give someone extra tickets to boost their chance.

    2. 2Let guests sign up

      Guest sign-up opens the form in a new window. Guests enter their name, phone number and email, tick consent and see “You’re entered into the raffle!”. They join the wheel straight away.

    3. 3Spin

      Press SPIN, tap the wheel or press Space. The winner is picked first with the browser’s secure random generator, then the wheel lands on them.

    4. 4Winner actions

      Remove from wheel, Keep on wheel, or No-show — draw again for the same prize. Every draw goes in the winner log with round, type and time.

    5. 5Download everyone’s details

      Download Excel (.xlsx) gives you a spreadsheet of everyone who entered: their name, phone number and email. CSV exports are in the Entries and Winners tabs.

    Shortcuts: Space spin · F event mode · Esc close / exit.
